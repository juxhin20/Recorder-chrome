Features

Everything a recording needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Click the icon, pick what to record, and you're live. Simple enough for anyone, sharp enough for tutorials and demos.

Tab, window or screen

Record just the page — no browser bars — or a whole Chrome window, or your full screen.

Full HD MP4

720p, 1080p or 4K at 30 or 60 fps. MP4 (H.264 + AAC) that plays on every phone, PC and editor.

PC sound

Your folder

Saves to Downloads by default, or straight into any folder you choose. Long recordings are written to disk as they go.

Install

Ready in a minute.

STEP 1

Download

Get the zip — free, no account — and unzip it.

STEP 2

Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).

STEP 3

Load it

Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

STEP 4

Pin & record

Pin Recorder to the toolbar, click it and press This tab.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes — free, open source, no watermark, no time limit and no account.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings are written straight to your computer. There are no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Where are the videos saved?

In Downloads / Recorder Chrome AppAlbania by default. In Settings you can choose any other folder — the Recordings page then shows that folder.

How do I record music from Spotify or another app?

Choose Window / screen, pick your screen and turn on Share system audio in Chrome's picker. On Mac this needs Chrome 141+ and macOS 14.2+; on Windows it works when sharing the whole screen.

Can I use my AirPods or headphones mic?

Yes. Pick it from the microphone list in the menu. If you unplug it, the recording keeps going with your default mic.

Does it work on Windows and Linux?

Yes — anywhere Chrome 116 or newer runs, including Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.
