Install

Ready in a minute.

STEP 1

Download

Get the zip — free, no account — and unzip it.

STEP 2

Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).

STEP 3

Load it

Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

STEP 4

Pin & record

Pin Recorder to the toolbar, click it and press This tab.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes — free, open source, no watermark, no time limit and no account.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings are written straight to your computer. There are no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Where are videos and screenshots saved?

In Downloads / Recorder Chrome AppAlbania by default, screenshots in its Screenshots folder, named like Screenshot example.com 2026-10-30_12-31-05.png. In Settings you can change the folder — pick Documents and it creates Documents / Recorder Chrome AppAlbania.

Can I screenshot a whole long page?

Yes. Full page scrolls the page for you and joins it into one image. Save it as PNG, or as a PDF split into A4 pages, ready to print or send.

Can I use my AirPods or headphones mic?

Yes. Pick it from the microphone list in the menu. If you unplug it, the recording keeps going with your default mic.

Does it work on Windows and Linux?

Yes — anywhere Chrome 116 or newer runs, including Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.
